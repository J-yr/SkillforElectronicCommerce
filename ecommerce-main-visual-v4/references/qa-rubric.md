## 中文评分表

先检查一票否决项：商品、品牌、型号、价格、活动规则或日期错误；未经授权改变商品、Logo、包装或法定标识；编造卖点、认证、折扣、评价或稀缺性；必需文案或语言版本缺失；交付规格错误；核心信息在目标尺寸下不可读。任一项出现即不能判为通过。

| 维度 | 分值 | 可观察标准 |
|---|---:|---|
| 信息准确与完整 | 25 | 与母本逐项一致，无遗漏、幻觉或语言错配 |
| 传播有效性 | 20 | 核心价值可被快速理解，CTA 与目标一致 |
| 信息层级与可读性 | 15 | 主次明确，缩略图和原尺寸均可读 |
| 商品与品牌完整性 | 15 | 商品可辨识，资产未变形，品牌气质一致 |
| 构图与视觉工艺 | 15 | 焦点、比例、对齐、间距、色彩、光影与合成自然 |
| 渠道与交付适配 | 10 | 比例、尺寸、格式、安全区、版本与命名正确 |

建议无否决项且达到 80/100 才进入可发布候选；任一核心维度低于该项 70% 时继续迭代。课程统一测试若另有阈值，以其为准并记录。

每轮记录：版本、观察问题、修改动作、复测证据、分数/结论。

## English Rubric

Hard fails: incorrect product/brand/model/price/rule/date; unauthorized changes to product, logo, packaging, or statutory marks; invented claims, certifications, discounts, reviews, or scarcity; missing mandatory copy/locale; wrong delivery specifications; unreadable critical information. Any hard fail prevents approval.

| Dimension | Points | Observable standard |
|---|---:|---|
| Information accuracy and completeness | 25 | Matches approved source; no omission, invention, or locale mismatch |
| Communication effectiveness | 20 | Core value is quickly understood; CTA supports the objective |
| Hierarchy and legibility | 15 | Clear order; readable at thumbnail and native size |
| Product and brand integrity | 15 | Recognizable product; intact assets; appropriate brand character |
| Composition and craft | 15 | Deliberate focus, proportion, alignment, spacing, color, light, compositing |
| Channel and delivery fit | 10 | Correct ratio, size, format, safe area, versions, naming |

Recommended threshold: no hard fail and at least 80/100. Iterate when a core category is below 70% of its available points. Follow and record any later course-defined threshold.

For each round record version, observed issue, change, retest evidence, and score/result.
