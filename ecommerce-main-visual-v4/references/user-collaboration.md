# 分阶段用户协作 / Staged user collaboration

## 中文

用于新设计、用户要求逐步看稿，或需要判断一次确认覆盖哪些决策时。这里的“接口”是带有具体评审材料的对话决策点，不是假设存在的按钮或API。局部修改按[迭代协议](iteration-protocol.md)路由，不重走全流程。

### 1. 先确定推进方式，不额外设置模式选择问卷

- **协作模式（默认）**：在尚未决定且影响后续返工的节点让用户介入。“做一张海报”“交付中英文版”只规定目标，不等于委托所有创意选择。
- **快速路径（明确委托）**：用户说“直接出图”“你决定，不用逐步确认”等时，在授权范围内自行决定并执行，简记假设和选择，不强加以下审批。已有文案和资产约束仍有效，缺失事实不得编造。
- **定向修改**：沿用当前母版与已确认项，直接执行明确的修改指令；只对真实冲突或新增取舍提问。
- “先看方案”“先别成稿”的要求持续有效，直到对应节点通过或用户改变流程。确认前可以做该节点的草图，不能借完整交付目标越级做高保真终稿。

### 2. 五个介入接口

顺序遵循依赖：内容 → 注意力 → 空间 → 样式 → 实际效果。每次只呈现当前需要决定的事项；已知项自动沿用，相邻节点可合并，但不能把尚未发生的后续决定写成已批准。

| 接口 | 先给用户看的材料 | 要解决的选择与提问示例 | 通过后锁定 / 下一步 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1 资料与文案** | 已知简报；已有/待提供/需要拟写的文案清单；素材与规格缺口。缺文案时先问是否已有，不先替用户编写。需要拟写时先给待批准稿。 | “目前缺主标题与活动信息：已有定稿、需要我拟写，还是本版不展示活动？”仅在确实缺失时问；价格与日期不能靠拟写补成事实。 | 锁定本轮必需字段、可改措辞、事实母本、规格与未决项。未决项可占位做独立探索，但不进入含这些信息的终稿。 |
| **G2 信息主次与视觉叙事** | 用实际字段列出一级/二级/三级信息、预期阅读顺序及理由；一句话说明商品如何与场景交互。给出推荐和必要的另一种取舍。 | “你希望第一眼先识别商品与优惠，还是先感受到品牌主张？”“商品是被使用、被拿起，还是被收纳？”只问会改变布局的分歧。 | 锁定主要信息组、次要支撑、辅助信息和场景动作；不锁定具体字体。若叙事仍难选择，可合并G3用草图判断。 |
| **G3 低保真版式** | 2–3张同规格、相近完成度的草图或线框，标出商品尺度、动作/接触/遮挡、文字区和视线顺序。全部必需文案有位置，未排全文时标注字段与长度。 | “推荐B，因为拿取动作与主信息区互不遮挡；A的商品更完整但动态较弱。选哪版，或保留哪两项组合？”不得只给方向名称或换色图。 | 选择版式、商品与环境关系、文字分区。只选B不等于批准尚未展示的全部字体、配色和最终成图。没有视觉工具时可给明确标为文字版的布局规格，并说明尚未验证视觉布局。 |
| **G4 真实文案与字块样式** | 在选中版式上排入完整真实文案，展示实际换行、主次字重、色块、价格与CTA组合；未定样式最多比较2–3个局部样张，避免重复整图生成。多语言至少预排最长文本。 | “保持B的构图，建议米黄底深绿字突出一级信息，卖点用低对比色块；标题用有动势的展示字，长文案用清晰正文字。确认这套样式，或指定要改的模块？”颜色与字形按项目决定，不固定为米黄绿。 | 锁定排版与样式方向，进入高保真制作。若G3已展示真实全文及这些样式并获认可，合并通过，不再问一次。排不下先重排，不擅删文案。 |
| **G5 首稿定向反馈** | 一个选定方向的首稿、缩略预览、与已确认方案的对应关系；列出已检查项和仍待验证项。围绕本轮最关键的1–2个目标反馈。 | “请重点看：商品与场景是否真的有交互？第一眼是否先看到约定的一级信息？”用户可保留、标注修改或认可进入余下适配与导出，不笼统只问“满意吗”。 | 保留认可项，反馈只重开依赖节点。母版稳定后完成所需语言适配与逐版验收；用户明确要求同时评审多语时并行展示，不单方面减交付。 |

**G5之后是执行责任，不是让用户替代质检。** 检查商品、Logo、全部文案、数字、日期、可读性、文件规格与实际导出。用户认可审美不能豁免错误；已授权适配或导出不再额外问“可以开始了吗”。必要检查未过则继续修复或标明待精修，未获得所需审美确认则保持可评审状态，不冒称终稿。

### 3. 每次只提交一个清晰的决策包

简短格式：**当前阶段与状态 → 已沿用的确认 → 本次预览/对比 → 推荐及代价 → 一个聚焦问题 → 确认后要做什么**。一般一次不超过两个紧密相关的决定；不是一次抛出全项目问卷。

需要用户选择时，使用当前可用且适用的提问方式，明确选项也接受自由反馈。提出问题后停在当前可评审物，不同时执行依赖答案的高保真步骤。未获答复不等于默认同意；可继续资产检查、母本核对等独立工作。依上级工具规则处理无法获得答复的情况。

### 4. 确认要带范围和版本

维护简短决策记录，可留在对话或已有项目记录中，不要求另建系统。字段：`节点 | 方案/版本 | 事项 | 状态 | 依据 | 未决/委托范围 | 下游影响`。状态使用：待确认、已确认、委托决定、需重开。用户确认、设计者判断和技术检查分别记录。

- “确认”只覆盖最近明确展示的决策包；没有回答的价格、日期或文案缺口仍然未决。若存在多个含义且会改变执行，问一个范围问题。
- “B版可以”确认B展示的布局选择；若B已完整展示文案与样式并且用户认可整版，可一并记录通过，无需人为拆成更多审批。
- “米黄绿色字块再多一点，字体风格化一点”是样式修改授权，直接重开G4并保留布局、文案和场景，不再索要相同授权。
- 用户改变主张、规格或事实时，标记受影响的下游结论需重开；不撤销不相关的已确认项。明确委托可跨过多个未定节点，但不能授权虚构商品事实。

### 5. 反馈回退到问题发生的层级

| 反馈/变化 | 返回哪里 | 如何避免无效迭代 |
|---|---|---|
| 文案不全、活动信息变化 | G1；再检查受影响的G2–G4 | 更新字段映射与占位，不用旧稿漏字继续精修。 |
| “一级信息不突出”“阅读顺序不对” | G2 → 受影响的G3/G4 | 先重新定义焦点，再调整分组、位置、面积和对比，不只把同一标题加粗。 |
| “改成登山”“产品要在包里/与环境互动” | G2/G3 | 明确叙事、接触关系、动作与主体可见度；保留仍适用的已批准文案。 |
| “换字块配色/字体” | G4 | 只改授权样式与必要邻接间距，保持文案和构图。 |
| “看不出修改”“还是不够明显” | 诊断是层级、结构还是样式问题，再回对应节点 | 给出父版与本版的具体差异和验收目标；重排授权下应改变分组或位置，不能把轻微缩放包装成重构。无法测量时只称设计观察。 |
| 商品结构、文字渲染或导出错误 | 制作与QA | 这是技术修复责任，不让用户重新选择已确认的审美方案。 |

验收目标应可观察，例如“商品名与价格合为一级信息组，卖点退为二级，日期独立可读”，而不是“高级一点”。只能把实际检查结果称为已实现；“三秒理解”没有真人测试只能是目标。连续无改善按迭代协议停止当前方法并回退。

### 6. 这次案例提示了什么，而不规定所有海报怎样做

在促销水杯案例中，可把商品识别与活动价作为一级、性能卖点作为二级、品牌辅助信息及日期作为三级；标语是否降级取决于该项目目标，CTA与价格组合。户外场景可比较收纳、拿取、倚靠等交互。品牌形象广告则可能以主张为一级。不要将NORI、登山、米黄绿、特定价格、双语或4:5固化为默认值。

## English

Use for new designs, staged-review requests or approval-scope decisions. These interfaces are conversational decision points with reviewable artifacts, not invented UI buttons or APIs. Route local edits through the [iteration protocol](iteration-protocol.md) rather than restarting the process.

### Mode and dependency order

Default to collaboration at unresolved decisions that would cause downstream rework. A request for a poster or bilingual deliverables is not blanket creative delegation. Explicit “produce directly / you decide / no step-by-step approvals” enables a fast path within scope, with assumptions recorded and factual safeguards intact. Clear local edits execute directly. Proposal-first preferences persist until their decision is resolved or the user changes the process; authorized sketches are allowed, premature high-fidelity finals are not.

Proceed from content to attention, space, styling and actual effect. Reuse confirmed inputs and merge adjacent decisions where useful; do not require five separate conversational rounds or claim unseen choices are approved.

| Interface | Reviewable material | Focused decision | Scope locked / next step |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G1 Inputs and copy** | Known brief, supplied/missing/to-draft copy, assets and specification gaps. Ask whether copy exists before inventing it; proposed copy is unapproved. | Resolve only missing information: existing wording, requested drafting or explicitly omitting an optional campaign. Prices and dates cannot be invented by copywriting. | Required fields, editable wording, factual sources, specifications and open issues. Placeholders permit independent exploration, not a final containing unresolved facts. |
| **G2 Hierarchy and narrative** | Actual fields assigned primary/secondary/tertiary levels, intended reading order and rationale; one sentence on product-scene interaction. Recommend a focus and explain a meaningful alternative. | Product/offer first or brand proposition first? Used, carried, retrieved or stored? Ask only choices that change the layout. | Priority groups, supporting information and scene action, not precise fonts. Merge with G3 when a narrative is easier to choose visually. |
| **G3 Low-fidelity layouts** | 2–3 same-spec, comparable-fidelity sketches showing product scale, action/contact/occlusion, text zones and reading path. Reserve space for every mandatory field; label lengths if not typeset in full. | Choose or combine genuinely different arrangements, with a recommendation and trade-offs; names or recolors alone are insufficient. | Composition, environmental relationship and text zones. B alone does not approve unseen styling. Without visual tools, label a text layout specification honestly and disclose visual verification is pending. |
| **G4 Real-copy and text-block styling proof** | Full real copy in the chosen layout: wrapping, weights, color blocks, price/CTA grouping. Compare at most 2–3 local styling samples if unresolved; preflight the longest locale. | Confirm or revise typography and blocks, not the entire scene again. Select colors and forms for this project, not fixed cream/green defaults. | Typesetting and styling direction, then high-fidelity production. Merge with G3 if it already showed and gained approval for this detail. Reflow instead of silently dropping copy. |
| **G5 Focused first-draft feedback** | One selected-direction draft, thumbnail, correspondence to decisions, completed checks and untested items. | Review the key 1–2 objectives, such as physical interaction and intended first-read information. Retain, annotate or approve remaining adaptation/export; do not only ask “satisfied?” | Preserve approved elements and reopen only affected dependencies. Stabilize the master before remaining locale production; show locales together when explicitly requested rather than reducing delivery scope. |

After G5, technical QA remains the designer's responsibility: assets, complete copy, numbers/dates, legibility, specifications and exports. Aesthetic approval never excuses errors, and technical success never establishes user approval. Execute authorized adaptation/export without another synonymous permission request. Unresolved checks require repair or an honest refinement status; unresolved required aesthetic decisions remain reviewable, not final.

### Decision packages and confirmation scope

Use a compact package: current stage/status → confirmed carryovers → preview/comparison → recommendation and cost → one focused question → next action. Usually ask no more than two closely linked decisions, not a whole-project questionnaire. Use an available, appropriate question mechanism and accept free-form responses. Stop dependent high-fidelity work while awaiting a decision, but continue independent asset/copy checks. Silence is not approval; handle unavailable responses under higher-priority tool instructions.

Keep a short conversation or project record: `node | proposal/version | decision | state | evidence | unresolved/delegated scope | downstream impact`. States: pending, confirmed, delegated, reopen-required. Separate user approval, designer judgment and technical verification.

- “Confirmed” covers the latest explicitly presented package, not unanswered facts. Ask one scope question only if consequential ambiguity remains.
- “B works” approves what B shows. If full copy and styling were shown and approved together, record both without artificial extra gates.
- “More cream/green blocks and stylized typography” authorizes a G4 edit; retain scene/layout/copy and do not re-request that authorization.
- New facts, goals or specifications reopen affected dependencies, not every past decision. Explicit delegation may span nodes but cannot authorize invented product facts.

### Targeted rollback

Missing/new copy returns to G1 and dependent G2–G4 checks. Weak hierarchy returns to G2 and affected grouping, placement and styling: merely emboldening the same headline may not solve it. A new scene or physical interaction returns to G2/G3 while preserving valid copy. Font/block-color feedback returns to G4 within scope. “It looks unchanged” requires diagnosis of hierarchy versus structure versus styling, a concrete parent/new difference and an observable acceptance target; do not label slight scaling as restructuring. Product deformation, rendering and export errors belong to production/QA, not repeated aesthetic selection.

Observable targets describe grouping, reading priority and legible supporting details. Claim only verified changes; without human testing, “understood in three seconds” is a goal, not a result. Stop non-improving retries under the iteration protocol.

For the promotional tumbler example, product identity and offer may lead, benefits support, and dates/brand details follow, with CTA grouped with price. Brand campaigns may lead with their proposition. Storage, retrieval or resting against gear are possible interactions, not universal defaults. Never hard-code NORI, hiking, cream/green, a price, bilingual output or 4:5 into unrelated work.
