# 视觉验收 / Visual acceptance

## 中文

每项使用通过、不通过、未检查、不适用，附素材/截图/文件属性等证据。未检查或未知不能计为通过；不适用说明原因。规则静态审阅与成图验收分开记录。

- A 内容事实：对照批准母本检查品牌、产品名、规格、全部必需卖点、价格币种、活动完整日期、CTA、语言及免责声明。无新增未经证实的内容。双语逐项映射；原母本缺失或冲突则待确认。
- B 资产：对照原件检查轮廓、结构、颜色、Logo形状/角度/位置与包装；按品类核对关键细节。检查人物手部、接触、边缘、阴影、反射、透视和场景光源。无原件不能完成资产验收。
- C 传播层级：确认主焦点及信息顺序，商品与核心价值易识别，价格和CTA易找。三秒识别若实际测试，记录受测者回答；未测试则不写“消费者三秒可识别”。
- D 排版：文字完整、不越界、无遮挡；行距、对齐、同级间距和安全区一致。品牌与可读性优先于斜带、圆弧等装饰规则。英文长文本自然重排。
- E 场景：道具不压过主角，物体接触稳定，风格符合品牌，色彩与材质有依据。非对称可有意图，不强制居中。
- F 缩略与语言：查看原图、指定宽度（未指定约300px）、手机情境。标题、商品、价格、币种、必需说明与CTA可识别。小字拥挤先重排，移走必需文案须有授权。两语言分别检查。
- G 技术交付：按本任务确认的比例、像素、格式、色彩模式、安全区、数量、语言和命名检查文件；保留版本与父版本。概念、候选与终稿状态准确。

阻断项包括事实错误、受保护资产误改、必需文案缺失、规格错误和核心不可读。未核验必要项也不能批准。只有所有适用阻断项核验通过才可作为候选；终稿须完成全部交付核验。评分不抵消阻断项；失败修复按主文件和迭代协议。

记录格式：检查ID、适用性、状态、依据、观察问题、下一步、复测证据。结论应可追溯到实际检查，而非仅复述提示词。

## English

For each check record pass, fail, untested or not applicable with source/screenshot/file-property evidence; justify exclusions. Unknown cannot pass. Keep rule review separate from artwork approval.

- A Facts: approved brand/product/specifications, all mandatory benefits, price/currency, full dates, CTA, locales and disclosures. No unsupported additions. Map bilingual fields; missing/conflicting copy stays unresolved.
- B Assets: compare source outlines, structures, colors, logo shape/angle/position and packaging. Check category-specific details, hands/contact, edges, shadows, reflections, perspective and lighting. Missing originals prevent asset approval.
- C Communication: verify focus, intended hierarchy, product/value recognition, findable price and CTA. A three-second user test needs actual recorded responses; do not invent consumer comprehension.
- D Typography: complete text, no overflow/occlusion, consistent spacing/alignment/safe zones. Brand and readability override decorative angle rules. Reflow long English naturally.
- E Scene: supporting props, stable contact, appropriate brand style/color/material. Intentional asymmetry is allowed.
- F Scale/locales: inspect native, specified thumbnail width (approximately 300px if absent) and mobile context. Check title, product, price/currency, mandatory text and CTA in each locale. Reflow first; relocating required copy needs authorization.
- G Delivery: verify confirmed ratio, pixels, format, color mode, safe area, quantity, locales and naming. Preserve versions/parents and accurate status.

Facts, asset alterations, missing mandatory copy, wrong specifications and unreadable critical content are blockers. Missing required verification also prevents approval. Candidates require every applicable blocker verified and passed; finals require all delivery checks. Scores cannot offset blockers. Log check ID, applicability, status, basis, issue, next action and retest evidence.

## V4补充 / V4 additions

实际成图验收增加：字体形态是否服务主张，字重/字宽/字距与中英搭配是否协调；是否有超出换色的创意组织；主焦点、第二层信息和行动提示是否相互竞争；背景是否掩盖商品边缘；装饰图标是否提供真实信息。缩略图必须实际查看，不能从原图推断。记录观察与依据，不把审美判断伪装为用户测试。

For actual artwork, review how font form supports the proposition, weight/width/spacing and bilingual pairing; meaningful originality beyond recoloring; competition between primary focus, secondary information and action; background interference with product edges; and whether icons carry useful information. Actually inspect thumbnails rather than inferring from full size. Distinguish design judgments from measured user results.
