# 字体、创意与层级 / Typography, creativity and hierarchy

## 中文

在新概念、字体探索或层级重构时读取。局部改价等简单任务不启动全套探索。新设计默认按[协作协议G2–G4](user-collaboration.md)逐步解决未定选择；仅在明确委托直接制作时自行选定并完成。已有确认不重复提问，局部修改不追加全流程审批。

### 决策顺序：层级 → 版式 → 字块样式

先把实际文案分为一级、二级、三级信息，说明第一眼应看见什么及为何服务传播目标；视觉焦点可由商品与一个信息组合承担，不让每条卖点都争第一。接着用低保真图比较2–3种实质不同的组织方式，呈现商品与环境的接触、遮挡和动作，不能仅用风景背景表示交互。选中版式后用真实全文验证密度，再比较尚未确定的字体、色块和对比方式。正文未排全不能称为排版已验证；草图已经展示并获批的样式不必重问。

### 字体：扩大选择空间，不堆叠字体数量

根据品牌、主张和阅读场景比较合适的两到三类方向，不默认所有海报使用粗宋或几何黑体。可考虑人文/几何无衬线、宋/衬线、窄体/宽体、圆体、手写/书法或定制展示字等；没有一类必须使用。说明字宽、字重、字距、笔画节奏与主张的关系，并给正文和数字搭配。

通常一套标题字体加一套信息字体已足够，可用同家族不同字重形成层级；这只是简化策略，不是两字体硬上限。手写与展示字优先用于短标题，价格、日期和长卖点优先可读。英文配对检查字面高度、粗细和气质，不机械强配同名字体。

实际指定字体前核对工具可用性、中文字符覆盖与适用授权。不确定时用字形描述作为方向，不谎称已使用某个字体文件或已获得商用许可。只使用生成工具时，不保证输出属于特定字体家族。

### 创意：让差异来自组织和主张

先用一句话说明画面怎样表达产品价值。需要探索时，方向至少在叙事、构图机制或主体与空间关系中有实质区别，例如“实物与负空间形成出行动线”和“简洁编辑式产品展示”；不能只换背景颜色、字体或装饰。

不要把巨型标题、完整风景、三枚圆形图标、底部促销条当默认套餐。可以使用其中任何元素，但必须说明它的功能。图标不增加理解就删减；未经证实的内部结构、功能演示、包装和性能不可为新意而虚构。用户只授权字体调整时，不顺便重做场景。

### 主次：先分配注意力，再分配面积

明确第一焦点、第二信息组和行动提示。商品首图通常产品优先；传播主张主导的品牌KV可以标题优先。结合用户目标说明取舍，避免产品与标题同时高对比、大体量抢第一。

结合主体尺度、局部明暗/色彩对比、留白、位置与背景细节共同调整，而非一律缩小标题。商品轮廓附近简化背景，控制道具和图标的权重，促销价格与CTA可合组，日期仍有独立可读空间。无统一占比公式；没有原图测量不能声称最佳百分比。

检查原尺寸及任务指定缩略图；必要时用灰度或轻度模糊辅助观察注意力分布，但这不能替代原色、文字可读性或真人识别测试。具体图像处理按环境允许工具执行，不为诊断擅用被禁止的方法。

### 输出和证据

简短记录选定字体方向、创意机制、预期阅读顺序，以及明确牺牲/保留的内容。实际出图后分别记录商品核对、文案核对、缩略图检查、文件尺寸和未决项。区分“用户反馈”“设计观察”“测量结果”。保留失败稿；只有真实复测才能说明新意、层级或识别是否改善，不把V4版本号当作质量证明。

## English

Read for new concepts, typography exploration or hierarchy redesign, not a simple price change. New designs default to resolving choices through [G2–G4 collaboration](user-collaboration.md); explicit direct-production delegation allows choosing and completing without gates. Do not re-request existing approvals or append the full process to a local edit.

Decision order: hierarchy → layout → text-block styling. Assign actual copy primary/secondary/tertiary roles, explain the first-read target and its purpose, and let the product plus one information group carry the main focus rather than promoting every benefit. Then compare 2–3 genuinely different low-fidelity compositions, showing contact, occlusion and action when relevant; scenic backgrounds alone do not establish interaction. Fit all real copy in the chosen layout before exploring unresolved fonts, blocks and contrast. Incomplete typesetting is not verified fit, and styling already shown and approved needs no repeated gate.

Compare two or three suitable font strategies based on brand, proposition and reading context. Consider humanist/geometric sans, serif, condensed/extended, rounded, handwritten/calligraphic or display forms as appropriate; none is mandatory. Explain width, weight, spacing, rhythm and body/numeral pairing. A headline family plus an information family is often sufficient, not a hard limit. Keep decorative forms mainly for short headlines and preserve price/date/benefit readability. Pair locales by visual scale, weight and character rather than names alone. Check availability, glyph coverage and relevant licensing before claiming a named font; use form descriptions if uncertain. Generated text cannot guarantee a specific installed typeface.

Express the creative mechanism in one sentence. Exploratory directions should differ in narrative, composition or product-space relationship, not just recoloring or adding decoration. Do not default to giant headline + scenery + three circular icons + bottom promotion bar. Each element needs a function. Remove unhelpful icons; never invent product internals, packaging or performance for novelty. Font-only authorization does not permit a new scene.

Set primary focus, secondary information and action cue first. Product-first often suits listing visuals, headline-first may suit brand campaigns; explain the trade-off. Adjust scale, local contrast, space, position and background detail together instead of universally shrinking headlines. Simplify around product edges, control prop/icon weight, group price/action if useful and retain legible date space. No universal size ratios. Inspect native and specified thumbnail views. Grayscale or blur can assist attention review only with permitted tools; neither substitutes for color, legibility or actual user testing.

Record selected font strategy, creative mechanism, intended reading order and trade-offs. After production record source-product comparison, copy check, actual thumbnail inspection, file properties and unresolved issues. Separate user feedback, design observation and measurement. Preserve failures; only real retesting establishes improvements, not a version label.
